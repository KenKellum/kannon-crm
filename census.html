Employee Census

To prepare accurate group quotes, your advisor needs some basic company information and a simple list of employees (and their dependents). This takes about 10 minutes — and you can upload a spreadsheet you already have.

You do not have to send us employee names. Carriers price on date of birth, gender, ZIP and who is related to whom — not on who someone is. An employee number is enough at this stage; names are only needed later, if you decide to enrol.

Social Security numbers are optional. Some level-funded carriers price more accurately with them, so include them if you are comfortable doing so — but a quote can be prepared without. They are encrypted the moment they arrive and are never shown in full on screen.

Please don't include bank details or pay. Those play no part in a quote, and if your payroll export contains them we will ignore those columns and tell you we did.

Eligibility & what you contribute

STEP 2 — HIPAA AGREEMENT

An employee census contains health-related information, so federal law (HIPAA) requires a signed agreement before we receive it. It legally binds us to protect your employees' information — review and sign below (about a minute).
